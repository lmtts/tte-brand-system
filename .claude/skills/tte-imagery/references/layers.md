# TTE Imagery Layers — Full Specification

## Overview

All four layers must share the same color grading philosophy and lighting ethos. What alternates between layers is **scale, perspective, and proximity** — not the brand's visual fingerprint.

A complete visual story for TTE uses all four layers in combination. When producing a series (social campaign, video sequence, print spread), recommend distributing across layers.

---

## Layer 1 — THE SCALE
*Where the Mission Occurs*

**Strategic purpose**: Communicate the impossible scale of the task. Make the viewer feel the weight of what 43% of the world being unreached actually looks like physically. The environment is the protagonist.

**Emotional target**: Awe mixed with urgency. The viewer should feel small but summoned.

### Technical DNA

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Shot type | Extreme wide, panoramic, or elevated |
| Focal length | 16–24mm full-frame equivalent |
| Subject position | Tiny silhouette, 5–15% of frame height, purposeful posture |
| Aperture | f/5.6–f/11 (deep focus — both person and landscape sharp) |
| Lighting | Golden hour primary, backlit rim secondary |
| Color grade | Deakins teal-orange OR TTE Standard earthy muted |
| Grain | Fine, Portra 400 simulation |
| Composition | Low horizon, significant sky / negative space for type overlay |
| Motion | Stillness preferred — person has arrived at the edge |

### Environments (Spiritual Biomes)
- Towering mountain ranges (Himalayas, Karakoram, Caucasus)
- Endless desert plains (Sahara, Gobi, Arabian)
- Dense urban canyons (megacity skylines, favela rooftops)
- Jungle or forest edges at dawn
- Arctic tundra or steppe

### What to Avoid
- Drone shots that feel touristic rather than purposeful
- Centered symmetrical compositions
- Landscapes without a human presence (removes mission context)
- Clean midday light with no atmosphere

### Copy-Ready Base Prompt
```
extreme wide angle 16mm, lone human silhouette 10% of frame height, towering mountain range or vast hostile landscape, golden hour amber directional light, long shadows, muted earthy desaturated palette, Kodak Portra 400 grain, deep focus f/8, significant negative space for text overlay, in the style of Chris Burkard adventure photography, photorealistic editorial, National Geographic award-winning quality, no tripod-perfect symmetry, no stock photo aesthetic
```

### Variations
**Desert**: replace `mountain range` with `endless Saharan sand dunes, scorched horizon line`
**Urban**: replace `mountain range` with `towering megacity skyline at dusk, concrete canyon`
**Jungle**: replace with `Amazon or Congo basin river bend, impenetrable jungle canopy`

## Layer 2 — THE STILLNESS
*Focus on the Protagonist*

**Strategic purpose**: Reveal the spiritual and psychological intensity of the person in the field. Calm within chaos. This is the prayer-warrior layer — the moment the Holy Spirit is received.

**Emotional target**: Concentrated power. The stillness is not peaceful — it is the eye of the storm.

### Technical DNA

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Shot type | Medium to medium close-up |
| Focal length | 50–85mm full-frame equivalent |
| Subject | Tack-sharp; eyes closed (prayer) OR direct camera lock |
| Aperture | f/1.4–f/2.8 (subject isolated, background separated) |
| Lighting | Overcast diffuse primary; rim halo backlight secondary |
| Color grade | NatGeo saturated (subject rich, background muted) OR TTE Standard |
| Grain | Fine to medium, Fujifilm simulation |
| Background | In motion or blurred — market, crowd, city, festival |
| Topographic overlay | Primary candidate for the red contour overlay graphic treatment |

### Subject Types
- South Asian woman in blue or gold headscarf, prayer posture
- Young believer in a crowded urban environment, eyes closed
- Missionary in field context, expression of focus and weight
- Local church leader, direct gaze of authority and calm

### What to Avoid
- Eyes open and looking off-frame (reads as distracted, not intense)
- Clean backgrounds (removes the "stillness amid chaos" tension)
- Smiling expressions (too comfortable — removes urgency)
- Perfect symmetrical framing

### Copy-Ready Base Prompt
```
85mm portrait lens f/1.8 shallow depth of field, South Asian woman in blue headscarf eyes closed in prayer, tack-sharp subject against motion-blurred crowded market background, stillness within chaos, overcast soft diffuse light, NatGeo saturated subject muted background, Kodak Portra 400 warm fine grain, in the style of Steve McCurry National Geographic portraiture, dignified not pitied, editorial photojournalism quality, no posed stock photo, no smile
```

### Variations
**Male subject**: replace with `Central Asian man mid-30s, concentrated expression, weathered features`
**Night version**: replace light with `single practical candle light, deep surrounding shadow`
**Missionary POV**: replace subject with `Western woman traveler, eyes locked on camera, raw determination`

## Layer 3 — THE POV
*The Raw Moment*

**Strategic purpose**: First-person immersion. Strip away the distance of observation and place the viewer directly inside the mission field. The camera is the person. The world presses in.

**Emotional target**: Adrenaline and vulnerability. Slightly unsettling. The viewer is not safe.

### Technical DNA

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Shot type | First person / semi-subjective, handheld |
| Focal length | 24–35mm full-frame equivalent |
| Subject | Hands, boots, windows, passports, instruments, door frames |
| Aperture | f/2.8–f/5.6, one tactile element in sharp focus |
| Lighting | Available / practical only — candlelight, dashboard, aircraft, window |
| Color grade | Lubezki natural (lifted shadows, organic) OR TTE Standard |
| Grain | Coarse, high ISO pushed feel — 3200 ISO simulation |
| Motion | Motion blur acceptable and desirable; slight handheld unsteadiness |
| Imperfection | Intentional — photographic noise, slight blur = authenticity |

### Strong POV Subjects
- View through dusty small aircraft window at remote jungle airstrip below
- Worn hand gripping a passport at a border checkpoint
- Looking out a taxi window at neon megacity streets in rain
- Boots stepping onto red dirt runway / mountain path
- Dashboard instruments of a bush plane or 4x4
- Visa stamp being pressed into a passport page

### What to Avoid
- Tripod-stable, perfectly framed compositions (defeats the POV intimacy)
- Drone aerials presented as POV (too detached)
- Subjects that require explanation — the POV object should read immediately
- Clean modern interiors — must feel like a field environment

### Copy-Ready Base Prompt
```
first person POV 28mm handheld, looking through scratched dusty small aircraft window at remote jungle airstrip clearing below, one worn hand gripping seat edge in foreground, motion blur on passing jungle canopy, high ISO coarse grain, natural available window light, Lubezki natural light grade lifted shadows, photojournalistic handheld instability, raw field dispatch aesthetic, in the style of Paolo Pellegrin Magnum, no studio, no posed, no clean aesthetic
```

### Variations
**Border crossing**: `handheld 35mm, worn passport held at camera level, customs officer blurred background, fluorescent overhead light, anxious energy`
**Urban transit**: `looking out rain-streaked taxi window at night megacity, neon reflections on wet glass, subject profile sharp in foreground orange cap`
**Mountain approach**: `low angle boots on rocky mountain trail, out-of-focus summit in background, harsh midday side-light, dust and grit`

## Layer 4 — THE HUD
*Mission Intel*

**Strategic purpose**: Deliver spiritual intelligence with emotional impact. Make population statistics human. The HUD frame is a brief on an actual person from an actual people group — not a data point on a map.

**Emotional target**: Confrontation. There is nowhere to look except directly at this person and what the data beside them means.

### Technical DNA

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Shot type | Extreme close-up portrait OR wide shot with significant dark area |
| Focal length | 85–135mm, compressed, minimal distortion |
| Subject | Specific weathered face — lined, textured, historically specific |
| Aperture | f/2.8–f/5.6, eye(s) in sharp focus |
| Lighting | Harsh side-light OR cold overcast (high contrast, revealing) |
| Color grade | Bradford Dark (underexposed, dark lifted blacks) OR TTE Standard desaturated |
| Grain | Visible, coarse — tactile skin texture is essential |
| Typography overlay | Space Mono typeface. Target name, estimated population, status, biome, gospel access %, coordinates |
| Desaturation | Slight additional desaturation to carry white type without fight |

### HUD Data Template
```
TARGET: [PEOPLE GROUP NAME]
EST. POP: [XX,XXX,000]
STATUS: UNREACHED
ACCESS: RESTRICTED / CLOSED / LIMITED
GOSPEL ACCESS: X.X%
BIOME: [ARID / SEMI-ARID / TROPICAL / URBAN / ARCTIC]
COORDS: XX°N, XX°E
```

### Subject Types
- Central Asian man, 50s–70s, deep facial lines, weathered by altitude
- North African elder, traditional headwrap, direct confrontational gaze
- South Asian woman, eyes partly visible over scarf, watchful expression
- One intense eye in extreme close-up — iris texture visible

### What to Avoid
- Generic faces that could be from anywhere
- Smiling or welcoming expressions (removes confrontation)
- Clean skin — texture is what makes the face real
- Symmetrical composition — asymmetry creates tension

### Copy-Ready Base Prompt
```
135mm extreme close-up portrait, weathered Central Asian man face 60s, one eye sharply focused with visible iris and skin pore detail, deep facial lines from altitude and sun, black wool balaclava or traditional headwrap, harsh side-light high contrast, dark Bradford Young underexposed aesthetic cold blue-grey sky background, coarse film grain 3200 ISO, Magnum photojournalism quality, ultra-detailed skin texture, editorial documentary portrait, not generic, not smiling, confrontational presence
```

### Variations
**Eye-only**: `extreme macro, single human eye, wrinkled skin surrounding, sclera veins visible, catch-light from overcast sky, grain and texture`
**Wide with dark panel**: `extreme wide, person tiny against landscape, 60% dark sky area top right for data overlay, low horizon`
